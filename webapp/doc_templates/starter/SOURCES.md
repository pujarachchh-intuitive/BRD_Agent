# Template content verification report

Prepared 2026-10-13. Covers `webapp/doc_templates/starter/healthcare.json` and `webapp/doc_templates/starter/financial_services.json`.

## Method

- **CFR text.** All CFR text was pulled from the official eCFR versioner API (`https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/<date>/title-NN.xml?part=...`). The point-in-time date was **2026-10-08**, which eCFR reported as "up to date as of" for titles 12, 16, 17, 31 and 45.
  - 45 CFR Part 164, 16 CFR Parts 313/314, 12 CFR Part 1016 and 31 CFR Part 1020 are textually identical between 2026-01-01 and 2026-10-08.
  - 31 CFR Part 1010 changed in 2026 only in the CTA beneficial-ownership *reporting* provisions (§ 1010.380). Those provisions are not used here. The CDD rule (§ 1010.230), CTR rules and § 1010.430 did not change.
- **Other official sources.**
  - The 2009 HHS Breach Notification interim final rule came from govinfo.gov. hhs.gov returned HTTP 403 to automated fetches.
  - The HIPAA Security Rule NPRM status came from the Federal Register API.
  - The FinCEN news release came from fincen.gov.
  - The OFAC Framework PDF came from ofac.treasury.gov.
  - The NIST publication pages came from csrc.nist.gov.
  - PCI material came from pcisecuritystandards.org, as described next.
- **PCI DSS.** The v4.0.1 standard PDF sits behind a license-agreement gate (docs-prv.pcisecuritystandards.org returned 403). The requirement wording was therefore checked against the PCI SSC's publicly hosted **PCI DSS v4.0 SAQ D for Merchants**, which reproduces the requirement text verbatim (https://listings.pcisecuritystandards.org/documents/PCI-DSS-v4-0-SAQ-D-Merchant.pdf).
  - The PCI SSC blog announcing v4.0.1 states: "There are no additional or deleted requirements in this revision" (https://blog.pcisecuritystandards.org/just-published-pci-dss-v4-0-1).
  - Requirement numbers are therefore the same in v4.0.1. v4.0.1 made only formatting, typographical and clarification changes.
  - **Residual risk:** minor wording clarifications in v4.0.1 were not checked word for word. The template paraphrases rather than quotes. The "best practice until 31 March 2025" notes in v4.0 have now expired, so those sub-requirements are mandatory.
- **HIPAA Security Rule proposal.** The NPRM "HIPAA Security Rule To Strengthen the Cybersecurity of Electronic Protected Health Information" (FR doc 2024-30983) was published 2025-01-06. As of 2026-10-08, the Federal Register API shows it only as a Proposed Rule, and the eCFR text is unchanged. It is not presented as a requirement anywhere.

Abbreviations used below:
- **eCFR(x)** means `https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-.../section-x`, the source_url in the JSON.
- **SAQ D v4.0** means the PCI SSC SAQ D document listed above.

## Deliberately left out, and why

### Healthcare

- **Anything from the January 6, 2025 HHS Security Rule NPRM.** This includes mandatory MFA and encryption, 72-hour restoration, asset inventories and annual compliance audits. It is still a proposal, not a final rule.
- **Audit-log retention period.** HIPAA states none. The 6-year rule applies only to required documentation, and the guidance and items say so explicitly.
- **Specific encryption algorithms or NIST storage and transport references from the HHS "unsecured PHI" guidance.** The guidance was verified in its 2009 Federal Register form: NIST SP 800-111 for data at rest; SP 800-52, 800-77 and 800-113, or FIPS 140-2 validated, for data in transit. I could not fetch the current hhs.gov version (HTTP 403), so I could not confirm whether it has been updated. It was left out to avoid citing a possibly outdated version.
- **The 2024 reproductive health care privacy provisions (including the 164.509 attestation).** They still appear in eCFR, but their legal status was affected by 2025 litigation that I did not verify from an official source. Left out.
- **Accounting of disclosures (164.528).** Verified ("six years prior to the date on which the accounting is requested"). Left out to stay within the item budget, because it only matters for systems that make non-TPO disclosures. Candidate for a later open question.
- **Physical safeguards (164.310) and workforce training (164.308(a)(5)).** These are organizational rather than system requirements.
- **Right to amend (164.526) and the HHS access-fee rules.** These are narrower and the fee guidance could not be fetched.
- **Specific session timeout values, uptime percentages, RTO/RPO.** None are stated by HIPAA.
- **State laws** such as California's CMIA and state breach laws. They were not researched; they are only flagged as an open question.
- **Information blocking exceptions and details in Part 171.** Only applicability was verified. Kept as an open question.

### Financial Services

- **Bank Interagency Guidelines (12 CFR Part 30 App. B, 12 CFR Part 364 App. B, and others) as concrete requirements.** Coverage depends on the regulator, so this is kept as an open question. The 36-hour incident notification timing in the banking agencies' rule was not verified in the text and is not stated.
- **Regulation S-P 2024 amendments (incident response program, customer notification timing).** Only the existence and scope of 17 CFR 248.30 were verified, not the deadlines. They are only mentioned in guidance.
- **FTC Safeguards penetration testing and vulnerability assessment cadence (314.4(d)(2): annual penetration testing; vulnerability assessments "at least every six months").** Verified but left out to keep the item count down. It also applies to the institution's program rather than one system. Easy to add later.
- **Customer due diligence "nature and purpose" and ongoing-monitoring elements (1020.210(a)(2)(v)).** Verified for banks only and partly covered by the SAR item. Left out to avoid implying the same rule for every institution type.
- **SAR dollar thresholds for non-bank institution types** (for example MSBs). Not verified, so only the bank figures are given and labelled as such.
- **CTR exemptions (1020.315) and the casino CTR rule (1021.311).** Only referenced as "subject to applicable exemptions" and "casinos follow separate rules".
- **Any rumored or proposed changes to the $10,000 CTR threshold.** The regulation text as of 2026-10-08 still says $10,000, which is what is used.
- **FinCEN AML/CFT program NPRM and investment adviser AML rule.** The latter is shown in eCFR as delayed until Jan. 1, 2028. Not final or effective, so left out.
- **Corporate Transparency Act beneficial ownership *reporting* (31 CFR 1010.380).** This is a company reporting obligation, not a financial-institution system requirement, and it was changed in 2026.
- **OFAC 50 Percent Rule and specific screening match thresholds.** These were not verified from primary text in this session.
- **PCI DSS requirements beyond the core set**, for example 6.4.3 and 11.6.1 for payment-page scripts, and 12.5.2 scoping. Left out for length. The PCI requirement text was verified from v4.0 SAQ D, not the gated v4.0.1 PDF.
- **Specific encryption algorithms, key lengths, TLS versions and log-review frequencies.** No source states them for these rules.
- **SOX IT general controls detail (for example COSO or PCAOB AS 2201).** Not verified. Only the SEC rule text is used.
- **State privacy and data security laws** (for example NYDFS 23 NYCRR 500). Not researched; only flagged in an open question.
